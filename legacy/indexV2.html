FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?+
Most websites and campaigns launch in 3–6 weeks; larger custom software or AI projects take 2–4 months. You'll get a clear roadmap with milestones during the strategy phase.
How is pricing structured?+
We offer fixed-price project packages and monthly retainers for ongoing growth work. Every engagement starts with a free consultation and a transparent, itemized quote, no hidden costs.
Who owns the code and assets after delivery?+
You do, 100%. All source code, designs, accounts and content created for your project are fully yours on final payment, with clean documentation included.
Do you provide maintenance and support after launch?+
Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support window, and our care plans cover updates, monitoring, security patches and continuous improvements 24/7.
Can you integrate AI into my existing business?+
Absolutely. We build AI chatbots, automation workflows and custom integrations on top of your existing tools, starting with a quick audit to find where AI delivers the highest ROI.
What technologies do you work with?+
Modern, proven stacks: React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), and leading AI models. We choose the stack that best fits your goals, not the trend of the week.
